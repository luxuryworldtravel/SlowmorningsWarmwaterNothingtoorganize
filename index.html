Beach and honeymoon planning, done properly

A beach holiday sounds simple until you start comparing resorts. One has a stunning pool and a rocky shore. Another has a perfect beach and a three-hour transfer. A good honeymoon planner knows the difference before you book, and that is the work we do for couples and families every week.

Our Washington, DC team at Luxury World Travel builds each trip around the way you like to relax. Some couples want an overwater villa with a private deck and a floating breakfast. Others want a quiet resort with a spa, snorkeling and a table by the sea at sunset. We match the property to the people, not the brochure.

We also look after the details that quietly decide whether a trip feels special: the right room category, arrival gifts, private dinners, seaplane or boat transfers and well-timed flights. Anniversary, babymoon or just a long-overdue rest, we plan it with the same care.

Island and beach destinations we recommend

Four places our clients love, and what each one does best.

Fiji Island Escape

Calm lagoons, thatched villas on stilts and famously warm hospitality. Fiji suits couples who want privacy, snorkeling and a relaxed pace without losing comfort.

Bahamas Luxury Escape

A short flight from the DC area, with long white beaches, grand resorts and turquoise water. Ideal for a quick luxury break or a family trip.

Barbados Beach Holiday

Palm-lined coves, bright blue surf and excellent food. Barbados blends easy beach days with culture, rum tastings and lively evenings.

Private Villa Escape

A pool-front villa a few steps from the sand. We arrange chefs, staff and transfers so your group enjoys the privacy of a home with hotel-level service.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit the Maldives?

The dry season from roughly November to April brings the sunniest weather and calm seas. Rates are higher, so we compare it with shoulder months for value.

How early should we book a honeymoon?

Six to twelve months ahead gives the best choice of villas and flights, especially for peak dates. Shorter notice can still work.

Are all-inclusive resorts worth it?

Often yes for families and groups, because costs are predictable. Couples who love dining out may prefer a half-board stay. We help you choose.

Can you arrange romantic extras?

Yes. We can arrange private dinners, spa days, sunset cruises, flowers and room décor before you arrive.

Do you plan destination weddings too?

Yes. We coordinate venues, guest room blocks and group travel, and can add a honeymoon to the same trip.
